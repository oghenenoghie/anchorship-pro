# Design direction

- Primary reference: Dutch Offshore Contractors for industrial corporate hierarchy, bold hero sections and project-led storytelling.
- Secondary reference: SeaSpeed Marine for vessel/service discovery and clear enquiry paths.
- Do not copy their source code, exact layout, brand assets, copy or proprietary imagery.
- Brand palette: deep navy #0B1D2A, ocean blue #176B87, mist #F3F5F6, white.
- Use strong but restrained typography, generous whitespace, clear calls to action, and accessible contrast.
- Prefer genuine, rights-cleared marine photography. Include meaningful alt text and avoid invented claims in overlays.
- Design mobile-first; test navigation, tables, forms and cards at small widths.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid animation that blocks reading or keyboard navigation.
- Use semantic headings, visible focus states, labelled form controls, and clear error/success announcements.
